# Employer confidentiality

Iago works at Hoom Interativa and OPER8. Their products, clients and prospects are not his to show. A first draft of a partnership document described employer products and client cases without names, and an independent review still traced every one of them.

**Why:** an unnamed but specific description identifies the client or product just as well as a name, especially to a reader who has worked near the employer.

**How to apply:**
- Free to show: the published websites listed in `src/content/projects.ts`, plus the one internal system the owner cleared (named in [[employer-confidentiality-details]]).
- Everything else from employer work gets described as a generic capability with no client, metric, product feature list or launch status. If no generic version exists, drop it.
- Never name the employer's products. Iago may build his own ideas outside, never the ones that exist at the employer.
- This repo is public: client and product names go only in `docs/memory/private/` (gitignored), never in a tracked file.
- Run [[independent-review-before-sending]] on the result.
